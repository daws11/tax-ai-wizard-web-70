Can a free zone entity with "qualifying income" still claim Small Business Relief?

No — the two regimes can't be combined. A Free Zone Person must apply the Qualifying Free Zone Person regime; Small Business Relief is not available alongside it for the same period.

Corporate Tax Law · Art. 18 Cabinet Decision No. 55/2023
