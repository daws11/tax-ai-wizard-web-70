Regulatory commitment

We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) Data Protection Regulations 2021 and applicable laws of the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

01

Introduction

This privacy notice ("Notice") for TAX-AI ("we," "us," or "our") explains how and why we collect, use, and process your personal information when you interact with our services, including through our website, mobile applications, or other channels.

02

What personal information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, login credentials.
  • User inputs — data you enter into our AI tools or upload as files.
  • Communication data — messages, inquiries, or feedback you submit.
  • Social media data — information you provide through interactions on our social media pages.
  • Sensitive information — we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

You are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date information.

03

Why we process your data

  • To operate and provide the TAX-AI services.
  • To manage user accounts and interactions.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve the platform.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We only process your data when it is legally permitted, such as under consent, legal obligation, or legitimate interest.

04

Sharing your information

We may share your personal data with:

  • Our group companies.
  • Service providers and contractors.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities within or outside the UAE.
  • Third parties involved in transactions or business changes.
  • Analytics or marketing partners (aggregated/anonymized only).

We ensure all third parties handle your data with appropriate safeguards.

05

Data storage & security

We store your data securely on protected servers and use technical and organizational measures to protect it from unauthorized access or misuse. While we take reasonable precautions, no system is fully secure, and you use our services at your own risk.

06

Children's privacy

TAX-AI is not intended for users under 18. If we learn that personal data from minors has been collected, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians can contact us to request deletion.

07

Data retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the stated purposes, legal requirements, or until you withdraw consent (if applicable). Typically, this is no longer than 12 months unless required by law.

08

Cookies and external links

We use cookies to improve user experience. Please refer to our Cookie Policy for more details. Our website may link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

09

Your rights

You have rights under applicable laws, including:

  • The right to be informed
  • The right to access
  • The right to rectify
  • The right to erase
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to object
  • The right to withdraw consent
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the ADGM Office of Data Protection

To exercise any of your rights or raise concerns, please contact us.

10

Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of TAX-AI after any updates signifies your acceptance of the revised Notice.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@taxai.ae.
